Quarterly Financial Report - Q4 2025

Executive Summary:
The fourth quarter of 2025 showed remarkable growth across all business segments.
Total revenue reached $45.2 million, representing a 23% year-over-year increase.
Net profit margin improved to 18.5%, up from 15.2% in Q3.

Key Highlights:
- Cloud services segment grew 35% to $18.5M
- Enterprise solutions contributed $15.2M, up 12%
- Professional services revenue was $11.5M, up 28%
- Customer retention rate reached 96.8%
- New customer acquisitions totaled 234 accounts

Regional Performance:
North America led with $28.4M in revenue (63% of total)
Europe contributed $10.8M (24% of total)
Asia-Pacific generated $6.0M (13% of total)

Outlook for 2026:
Management expects continued growth in cloud services and enterprise solutions.
The company plans to expand into new markets in Southeast Asia and Latin America.
Capital expenditure for 2026 is projected at $12M for infrastructure upgrades.

Risk Factors:
- Increasing competition in cloud services
- Currency fluctuation exposure in international markets
- Regulatory changes in data privacy across EU markets
